# Resume Bullets — Retail Sales & Outlet Performance Analytics

## Recommended project entry

### Retail Sales & Outlet Performance Analytics | Python, Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn, Jupyter

- Analysed **8,523 item-outlet records** using Python and Pandas, building KPI and segmentation views across 16 product categories, 4 outlet formats, 3 location tiers, and 3 outlet-size groups.
- Standardised inconsistent categorical labels, validated duplicates, missingness, ranges, and negative sales, and documented **1,463 missing item-weight values** without unsupported imputation.
- Compared total sales with average sales per record and correlation signals to identify scale-versus-efficiency patterns, including Supermarket Type1 as the total-sales leader and Supermarket Type2 as the average-sales leader.

## Short version

Analysed 8,523 retail records in Python/Pandas; cleaned categorical fields, validated data quality, calculated sales KPIs, compared outlet and product segments, and translated descriptive findings into business recommendations with documented limitations.
